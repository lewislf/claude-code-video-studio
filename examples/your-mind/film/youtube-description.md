# Title

Who holds the key to your mind?

# Description

In 2013, at MIT, scientists wrote a false memory into a mouse's brain using light. It became afraid of a box where it had never been shocked.

This video opens four files: writing, mapping, reading and influencing the mind. It separates what science has already done from what is still fiction (Severance, The Matrix, Roko's Basilisk) and ends on a question: who has the key?

Coursework for the Computers and Society class, UFES.
